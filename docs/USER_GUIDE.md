# User guide

## Home feed

The default `For You` feed combines urgent local safety items with relevant jobs, scholarships, services and learning posts. Every card displays its source, publication time, last-check time, verification state and category.

## Opportunities

Filter jobs, scholarships and learning resources. A recommendation score is a derived relevance estimate, not proof of eligibility or selection. Always open the original official link before applying.

## Disaster and weather

High-severity local alerts are prioritised. LifeBridge AI is decision support; follow local authorities and emergency services for instructions.

## Trust scanner

Paste a suspicious message or URL. The response lists detected evidence and safer next actions. A low score does not guarantee that content is legitimate.

## Services

With location permission, the app queries nearby hospitals, clinics, shelters and selected support services. OpenStreetMap completeness varies by country and locality.

## Privacy

Do not upload passwords, one-time codes, full card numbers or unredacted identity documents. Use the profile controls to minimise stored personal data and delete content you no longer need.
