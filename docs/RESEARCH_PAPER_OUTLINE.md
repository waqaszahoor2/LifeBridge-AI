# Suggested final-year research paper outline

1. **Introduction:** global information fragmentation across opportunities, safety and accessibility.
2. **Related work:** recommender systems, multilingual NLP, scam detection, geospatial decision support, explainable AI and knowledge graphs.
3. **Problem definition:** unified relevance–accessibility–safety–trust decision framework.
4. **Data and ethics:** official sources, synthetic baselines, provenance, privacy and source terms.
5. **System design:** web/mobile/API/worker/database architecture.
6. **Models:** skill matching, trust classifier, risk model and recommendation fusion.
7. **Experiments:** temporal/entity splits, ablations, calibration and subgroup evaluation.
8. **User study:** risk score alone versus evidence-linked explanations.
9. **Results and error analysis:** false positives/negatives, language and location gaps.
10. **Limitations:** source coverage, data drift, missing accessibility data and no guaranteed real-world impact.
11. **Conclusion and future work:** validated provider integrations, multilingual datasets, graph learning and field trials.
