# Claim and evidence policy

LifeBridge AI labels information according to its origin:

- **Source fact:** directly stated in an official source record, with its URL and publication time.
- **Derived result:** calculated by the platform, such as distance, risk score or match score.
- **Human verified:** reviewed by an administrator against the original source.
- **Community/unverified:** submitted but not confirmed.
- **Demo:** synthetic content used only for testing.

## Examples

- “Deadline: 30 September” is a source fact only when present on the official programme page.
- “92% match” is a derived recommendation score, not an acceptance probability.
- “High flood risk” from an official alert must name the provider; output from the synthetic baseline must be labelled as a model demonstration.
- “Verified” means source identity/details were checked; it does not guarantee future validity or user eligibility.

The system must display uncertainty, timestamps and original links rather than making universal safety or success claims.
